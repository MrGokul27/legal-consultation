The Chambers Narrative

Founded on the Principle of Relentless Legal Fidelity

In 1996, Sir Alistair Montgomery KC established Stackly Legal in Bedford Row, Holborn, with a resolute objective: to deliver fearless courtroom advocacy and strategic corporate counsel untangled by institutional bureaucracy.

Over the last thirty years, our practice has grown from a boutique litigation set into a premier multi-disciplinary firm advising FTSE enterprises, sovereign entities, high-growth innovators, and distinguished families across the globe.

"Our reputation rests on forensic thoroughness. We leave no stone unturned in preparing a brief, ensuring our clients enter negotiations or courtrooms in an unassailable position."

— Eleanor Kensington Esq., Managing Partner
